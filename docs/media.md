## Refresh captures

Use one Simulator with the intended release build. Capture the app itself without browser controls or debug overlays. Show a short, understandable interaction: move on the map and open a discovery; search and open a structure; or swipe between tour stops. Check the first and last frames and avoid showing temporary permission dialogs unless they are the subject.

Keep original captures alongside composed App Store exports in the release workspace. Record the app version and source revision so website media can be checked against the submitted build. Share approved exports with the separate website project; do not add its video pipeline to the app.

The earlier campaign files and Photoshop originals are in [promotional/archive](../promotional/archive/README.md). They are historical and must not be reused as current screenshots.
